## Corrections after the independent review (24 October 2026)

A fresh reviewer, who had not seen the work being produced, checked every quoted number against the results files, reran the evaluation (it reproduced exactly) and read the brief. It found no wrong headline but these issues, all fixed:

1. **Inner threshold selection was not isolated as the protocol requires.** Inner folds reused the outer-fold offset prior, which included the inner held-out patients. Each inner fold now recomputes the prior from its own fitting patients and recalibrates. Effect: the learned scores' thresholds fell by 0.5 to 1.5 mg/dL and the hybrid alert went from 36 to 35 of 53; the simple rule and every forecast were unchanged.
2. **Replay parity had been tested at one origin.** Testing 13 origins found a mismatch for insulin-pump patients. In the full record a basal rate could run past its 6-hour cap when a later record existed, which is future information, and the last pulse before a cut was dropped. Pulses now depend only on earlier records (guarded by a new test), and the evaluation was rerun. Effect: the physiology features of three pump periods changed, every model was refitted, and forecast errors moved by at most 0.1 mg/dL (2-hour hybrid 32.7 to 32.6; 30 and 60 minutes 13.3 and 22.9 to 13.2 and 23.0). The hybrid alert fell from 35 to 29 of 53 at the 0.5 budget (42 to 41 at 1.0); the simple rule stayed at 43 and 47. Sensor-off RMSE moved from 29.4 to 29.6.
3. **Prediction code is now shared** (`twin/inference.py`), as MUST-5 asked; the evaluation previously called the scikit-learn wrapper and the replay the booster.
4. **Double rounding.** Metrics are stored with four decimals and formatted once; the README's result tables are generated.
5. **Wording.** "Held-out after a 5-day calibration" instead of "never seen"; the 294 to 99 sensor result is a whole-period descriptive correction, not the twin's calibration-only method; "7 of 53" counts paired readings, not episodes; the physiology gain is quoted with and without the clinical record; the record is slightly worse at 30 and 60 minutes; "ready for a clinical pilot", "tests prove" and "reproduces exactly" removed; the README's public-access section now says the links are pending; the deck's rerun commands use `scripts/run_all.py`.
6. **Manifest.** Code hashes are now taken before the run, and the manifest lists any file that changed during it (none).
